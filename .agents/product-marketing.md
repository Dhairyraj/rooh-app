# Rooh - Product Marketing Strategy

## Brand Identity
- **Name**: Rooh
- **Tagline**: "Where souls meet, stories begin."
- **Brand Voice**: Intimate, emotional, profound, romantic, never corporate. Treat the user as a human seeking deep connection, not a "user".

## Target Audience
- **Demographic**: Indian couples, ages 20-35.
- **Psychographic**: Value meaningful relationships, looking for ways to break out of routine, willing to invest in experiences and memories over physical goods. They might feel disconnected despite spending time together.

## Visual Aesthetics
- **Palette**: Deep Plum (`#2A0E1D`) and Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`).
- **Vibe**: Cinematic, elegant, premium, warm, inviting.

## Pricing Tiers
- **Spark**: Free (Taste of deep connection, first 12 questions of 36 Questions)
- **Flame**: ₹299 (Unlock all games, Compatibility Engine, Love Language)
- **Eternal**: ₹999 (Digital memory website, Time Capsule, Anniversary Countdown, priority generation)
- **Forever**: ₹1999/yr (Subscription model for ongoing relationship growth)

## Target Keywords (SEO)
- couples games online
- relationship intimacy questions
- online memory capsule for couples
- digital anniversary gift india
- 36 questions to fall in love game
- compatibility test for couples
- love language decoder quiz

## Banned Words
Do NOT use the following words anywhere in the copy (they sound generic/corporate):
- premium
- seamless
- innovative
- cutting-edge
- state-of-the-art
