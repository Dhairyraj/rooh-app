# Rooh Pricing & Tiers

Rooh is a digital intimacy platform for couples.

## Spark Tier (Free)
- A taste of deep connection
- Access to the first 12 questions of the 36 Questions game
- Price: Free

## Flame Tier (₹299)
- Full access to all games
- The complete 36 Questions of Love
- Compatibility Engine
- Love Language Decoder
- Intimacy Jar
- Price: ₹299 (One-time purchase)

## Eternal Tier (₹999)
- Everything in Flame Tier, plus:
- Digital Memory Website generator
- Digital Time Capsule
- Anniversary Countdown Dashboard
- Price: ₹999 (One-time purchase)

## Forever Tier (₹1999/year)
- Subscription model for ongoing relationship growth
- New questions and games added monthly
- Priority support and early access to new features
- Price: ₹1999 per year
